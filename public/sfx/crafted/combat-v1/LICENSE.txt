Blade Surge Combat Sound Library V1

These twelve non-vocal effects were synthesized from mathematical oscillators
and seeded noise by tools/audio/build-combat-library.py. No third-party audio
recording, paid service, or voice source was used.

To the extent possible under law, the authors dedicate these generated sound
effects to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Existing character voice performances are separate assets and are unchanged.
