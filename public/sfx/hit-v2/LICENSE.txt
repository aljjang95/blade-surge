Blade Surge Hit Sound Library v2

These effects are edited (trimmed, filtered, layered, pitch-shifted) from Kenney "Impact Sounds" and
"RPG Audio" (CC0 1.0) plus oscillator/noise layers synthesized by tools/audio/build-hit-library.py.
Sources: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds , https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
Original file names and SHA-256 hashes are in manifest.json (sourceFiles / derivedFrom).

To the extent possible under law the edited result is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Character voice performances are separate assets and are unchanged.
